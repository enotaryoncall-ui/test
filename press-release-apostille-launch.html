eNotary On Call Launches Worldwide Apostille Services, Expanding Global Document Authentication for Law Firms and Businesses

New offering delivers fast, compliant apostille and international document authentication support across all Hague Convention member countries, built for law firms, corporate legal departments, and professional service organizations.

CITY, STATE — Month DD, YYYY — eNotary On Call, a trusted provider of remote online notarization (RON) and document authentication services, today announced the nationwide and international launch of its Apostille Services, extending its support to businesses and legal professionals who require authenticated documents for use in foreign countries.

eNotary On Call today announced the launch of its worldwide Apostille Services, a new offering designed to help law firms, corporate legal departments, and professional service organizations authenticate documents for use anywhere in the world. The service extends the company's existing remote online notarization platform with end-to-end apostille processing, giving legal teams a single, reliable partner for both notarization and international document certification.

An apostille is a form of authentication issued for documents used in countries that are members of the Hague Apostille Convention, confirming that a document is legitimate and can be legally recognized in another member country. Historically, obtaining an apostille has required firms to navigate multiple state and federal agencies, track inconsistent turnaround times, and manage the process manually — a workload that pulls staff away from client-facing work.

With this launch, eNotary On Call is positioning itself as an outsourced apostille processing partner for law firms and businesses that need to reduce administrative burden, support international clients, and ensure documents are processed correctly the first time.
